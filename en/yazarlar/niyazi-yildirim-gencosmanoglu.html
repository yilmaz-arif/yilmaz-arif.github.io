Author dossier

Niyazi Yıldırım Gençosmanoğlu

1929–1992

Work on Niyazi Yıldırım Gençosmanoğlu goes back to an MA thesis completed at Gazi University in 1996. A monograph on the poet’s life and poetic art followed in 2000. His books of poetry appeared in a critical edition as Bütün Şiirleri (Collected Poems) from Türk Edebiyatı Vakfı Yayınları in 2002, and again in 2023 as four volumes from Bilge Kültür Sanat. In addition to these publications, the dossier includes a video of the poet reading his own poems, a handwritten poem draft and one of the letters Hüseyin Nihâl Atsız addressed to him.

Commemoration (2011)

The program “Şair Niyazi Yıldırım Gençosmanoğlu’nu Anma” (In Memory of the Poet Niyazi Yıldırım Gençosmanoğlu) was held on 9 March 2011 in the Ömer Asım Aksoy Conference Hall of Gaziantep University. It was prepared jointly by İLESAM, the Professional Union of Owners of Scientific and Literary Works, and Türkçe Topluluğu, a student society of the university’s Directorate of Culture, and was chaired by the society’s academic advisor, Arif Yılmaz. In his talk Yılmaz placed Gençosmanoğlu among the greatest epic poets of Turkish literature. The poet’s son, Talat Gençosmanoğlu, also spoke about his father. The program continued with poems read by students of the society and a recital of the poet’s poems set to music. Two days later a second commemoration took place at the Gaziantep branch of İLESAM.
